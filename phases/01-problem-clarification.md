# Phase 1 — Problem Clarification

**Goal:** turn the raw hackathon prompt into one precise, chosen problem framing before any
scoping or building starts. Run this after `kickoff.md` exists (Phase 0) — time budget, team,
and deadline are already settled by this point.

## Steps

1. Restate the given problem/prompt in your own words. If it's genuinely ambiguous, say so
   explicitly rather than silently picking an interpretation.
2. Identify:
   - **Target user** — who specifically has this problem?
   - **Core pain point** — what breaks or is slow/expensive/annoying for them today?
   - **Judging criteria**, if stated (innovation? technical depth? business viability? social
     impact? use of a sponsor's API?) — these should shape what gets built, not just the demo.
   - **Constraints** — required or bonus-eligible sponsor technologies, any rules on what's
     allowed (pre-written code, specific APIs, etc). Time budget and team composition are
     already in `kickoff.md`.
3. Generate 2–3 plausible problem framings/angles on the same prompt (different target user,
   different core pain point, or different scope of the problem).

## Decision point

> **Which framing to pursue** is the decision. Present the framings with a one-line tradeoff
> each (e.g. "broader user base but shallower technical story" vs "narrow niche but better fits
> the sponsor track").

## Output

`problem-statement.md`:
- Chosen framing + why
- Target user, core pain point
- Judging criteria and constraints noted
- The framings that were *not* chosen, and why (useful later for the pitch's "why this
  approach" section)
